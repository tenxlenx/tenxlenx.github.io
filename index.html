Personal desktop utility

Read account verification emails on your own device.

OpenBot Mail Verification reads recent verification messages from the owner's Gmail account to complete sign-ins that the owner has requested.

How it works

You authorize read-only Gmail access in Google's browser consent screen. The locally installed utility then looks for recent verification messages from the supported account service and processes the verification code or device approval link on your computer.

Device approval is used only when explicitly enabled for the requested sign-in. Gmail access does not send, delete, label, or mark messages as read.

Your account, your control

This is a personal-use installation managed by tenxlenx. This website is informational and has no sign-in form. It does not receive your email messages or OAuth credentials.

You can revoke access at any time through your Google Account connections. Read the privacy policy and terms of use for data handling and support details.
